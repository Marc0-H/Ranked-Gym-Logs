RANKED GYM LOGS

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Ranked Gym Logs is a workout-tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information handled by the app

The app lets you enter and view:

  • Workout dates, exercises, sets, repetitions, and weights
  • Bodyweight measurements and dates
  • Your selected body type and unit preference

How information is stored and used

Ranked Gym Logs stores this information locally on your device in its on-device database. It uses the information only to provide workout logging, progress tracking, and strength-ranking features.

The app does not create user accounts. It does not send your workout data, bodyweight, settings, or identifiers to a developer-controlled server. It does not sell or share this information with third parties, and it does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Your device operating system or backup provider may handle app backups according to the settings you choose for your device and account. Ranked Gym Logs does not control those device-level backups.

Your choices and data deletion

You can change your body type and unit settings in Profile & Settings. You can delete workout history in the app's Profile & Settings area.

To remove all data stored by the app, clear the app's storage through your device settings or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes the app's locally stored database from the device, subject to any device-level backups you may have enabled.

Children

Ranked Gym Logs is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's data practices change. The latest version will be available at this page and within the app.
